Technology watch

Chosen watch topic

Cloud-managed networks: Cisco Meraki, SD-WAN and SASE.

With these solutions, network devices (Wi-Fi access points, switches, firewalls) are configured and monitored from an online dashboard, without logging in to each device. SD-WAN links a company’s sites over the Internet by picking the best path, and SASE adds security services delivered from the cloud on top.

Why this topic: it is at the heart of UBNEST’s business, which installs Cisco and Meraki solutions for its clients. It is also where networking and cybersecurity meet: a remotely managed network still has to be updated and protected.

Watch tools used

  • Google Alerts: alerts on “Meraki”, “SD-WAN”, “SASE” and “Cisco vulnerability”
  • TLDR: daily tech newsletter
  • Cisco security advisories: official publications of flaws and fixes
  • Cisco Meraki community: new feature announcements

Summaries and reading notes

October 2026

Cisco fixes seven flaws across the whole Meraki range

On 7 October 2026, Cisco published a security advisory covering seven vulnerabilities in six Meraki product families: Wi-Fi access points, switches, security and SD-WAN appliances, cameras, cellular gateways and Campus Gateways. The most severe scores 9.6 out of 10. There is no workaround: only a firmware update fixes the issue, and some fixes were only expected in October or November. Cisco knew of no exploitation when it published.

My takeaway: Even when managed from the cloud, devices still have to be updated. For a technician, that means following security advisories, checking the versions installed at each client and planning updates.

Sources: Cisco Security Advisory · eSecurity Planet (FR)

September 2026

SASE keeps growing and SD-WAN picks up again

According to Dell’Oro Group, the SASE market reached $3.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026, growing more than 20% year on year for the fifth quarter in a row. SD-WAN is accelerating, driven by the modernisation of branch networks (offices, shops, branches).

My takeaway: Multi-site businesses increasingly want a single solution that handles both site connectivity and security. Understanding SD-WAN and SASE is becoming useful for a network technician.

Sources: Dell’Oro Group

August 2026

AI comes to Meraki network administration

Cisco added an AI assistant to the Meraki dashboard that can chain actions together (so-called “agentic” capabilities). On 10 August 2026, the company also presented MCP servers that let AI tools access Meraki and Catalyst Center data.

My takeaway: Network administration is becoming automated. Technicians will need to check what the AI does and limit the rights it is given: that is a security question too.

Sources: Cisco Meraki Feature Announcements · Network-Switch.com
